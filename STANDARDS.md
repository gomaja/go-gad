# Standards and scope

Versions checked against the 3GPP specification archive on **2026-10-15**. The

| 3GPP TS 23.032, *Universal Geographical Area Description (GAD)* | V19.0.0 | §6 coding, §7.2 Table 2a, §7.3 shapes, §8 velocity, Annex A | [23.032](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/23_series/23.032/) |

TS 23.032 codes eleven shapes in octets (§7.3) and four velocity types (§8,
Table 3). The shapes of §5.8 to §5.12 (local and relative locations, range and
direction) and the relative velocity of §8.4a have no octet coding there and
are out of scope.

The field layouts of the high-accuracy shapes (§7.3.3a, §7.3.3b, §7.3.6a and
§7.3.6b) exist only as figures; Annex A gives the compact notation of the other
shapes and of the velocity types.
